Adds helpful vendor settlers to your settlement:

• Trinket Vendor — drop/chest trinkets; stock and prices unlock with boss progress
• Potion Vendor — potions unlock with boss progress
• Vinyl Vendor — music vinyls and a portable music player
• Exotic Merchant — same shelf as the vanilla exotic merchant, refreshed daily

Trinket and Potion vendors become recruitable visitors after defeating the Pirate Captain.

Compatible with Necesse 1.3.3.
