Chain-mine connected ore veins (and optional extras).

• Enabled by default
• V — toggle vein mining on/off
• K — open config (all ores, all trees, extra list, chain limit)
• Drop ore items to chain that ore; drop stone blocks to chain those rocks; drop logs to chain those trees
• Trees chain a nearby patch
• Uses the same idea as vanilla cluster mining

Compatible with Necesse 1.3.3.
Author: Origami
Language: English / 简体中文
Controls are remappable in Settings.
